Who We Are

DriveEasy is a car rental website for the city of Jeddah. We started the idea after we noticed how hard it is for visitors, students and families to compare rental offices and to know the real final price before they sign the contract.

Our website shows every car with its photo, specifications and daily price, and lets the customer book online and pick up the car from one of our three branches.

Mission & Values

Our mission: to make renting a car fast, clear and safe for everyone in Jeddah.

Our values

  • Honesty – the price you see is the price you pay.
  • Quality – all cars are less than two years old and checked before every rental.
  • Customer care – support by phone and WhatsApp 24 hours a day.

Rental Policy

  1. The minimum rental period is one day (24 hours).
  2. The car must be returned with the same fuel level.
  3. Late return of more than 3 hours is charged as one extra day.
  4. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the pick-up time.
  5. Traffic violations during the rental period are paid by the customer.
