The Idea of DriveEasy

Renting a car in Jeddah usually means calling many rental offices, waiting in long queues and asking again and again about the price, the insurance and the car documents. Visitors who land at the airport and people whose own car is at the workshop often lose hours before they get a car, and many of them find extra fees only when they return it.

DriveEasy solves this by putting everything online in one place. The customer can see all available cars with real photos, compare the daily prices, choose the pick-up branch and the dates, add extras like GPS or a child seat, and see the total price before booking. The car is ready when the customer arrives at the branch.

Why Rent With Us?

  • Clear prices: the total cost is shown before you confirm, with no hidden fees.
  • Saves time: book from home in less than five minutes.
  • Many choices: sedans, SUVs, sports cars and pickup trucks.
  • Three branches:
    • King Abdulaziz International Airport
    • Al-Rawdah District
    • Al-Hamra District (Corniche)
  • Full insurance option and 24/7 road assistance.

How It Works

  1. Create a free account from the Sign Up page.
  2. Browse our cars and choose the one you like.
  3. Fill the booking form with the dates and the branch.
  4. Pick up the car from the branch with your ID and driving licence.
